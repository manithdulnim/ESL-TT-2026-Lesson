PAIR SPEAKING

Can You Come for Dinner?

Prepare for 1 minute with your partner, then act out the conversation without reading!

🏠 Student A — Host

  • Invite your friend
  • Explain what food you are preparing
  • Ask whether they have any dietary preferences
  • Tell them what ingredients you need
  • Ask them to bring something

🎁 Student B — Guest

  • Accept or refuse the invitation
  • Ask what food is being served
  • Offer to bring something
  • Ask where the dinner party is taking place

Speaking Mission Checklist (Tick as you include them!):

A: Would you like to come over for dinner on Saturday?

B: I'd love to! What are you preparing?

A: I'm making a chicken curry. I need a few vegetables and a little coconut milk.

B: Do you need anything else?

A: Could you bring a dessert?

B: Of course! I'll bring an apple pie.

A: Wonderful! Everyone is invited. The dinner starts at seven.
